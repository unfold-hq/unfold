Take on lists proposed, taken-over and running work in one list for your phone, with Approve, Hand back and Stop a thumb can reach.

Open it from the bottom bar on a phone, or from the sidebar and the command palette. Each row names the Team, what the work is and what Ploeg reports it costs. Every button opens the same confirmation as Proposed and the Work Item page, and the demo says that nothing is dispatched or spent.
