Administrators connect ClickUp on Settings → Connect ClickUp and bind Lists to a Team; Ploeg keeps the member's token and Unfold keeps none.

The page explains why Unfold needs its own ClickUp member and how to make it, hands that member's token (or a ClickUp sign-in, where the installation has a ClickUp OAuth application) to Ploeg once, and binds a List only to repositories Ploeg reports ready, after a dry run. A List counts as done only when Ploeg reports its binding active. Until the connected Ploeg serves these steps, the page says which Ploeg release adds them and sends nothing.
