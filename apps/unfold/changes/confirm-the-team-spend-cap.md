Administrators confirm each Team's spend cap on the Budget line of Settings → Connections before onboarding starts paid work.

Unfold shows the cap in US dollars per day, week or month as Ploeg reports it and records who confirmed it and when. A Team without a cap gets no readiness work and no List bound during onboarding; handing off tasks from sources set up in the configuration works as before.
