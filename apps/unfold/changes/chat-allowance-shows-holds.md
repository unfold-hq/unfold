The Agents window's chat says how much of a Team's chat allowance is only held, and Your usage counts what your chat messages actually cost.

Ploeg holds a fixed amount for each chat message and settles it to the actual cost later, so every message used to look as if it cost exactly that hold. The line after each answer and the Team picker now read, for example, `$1.85 of $2.00 chat allowance left ($0.10 held until settled, at most $0.05 per message)`. Your usage and `unfold_my_usage` list your chat messages with the cost Ploeg reports for each, marked not final until it settles.
