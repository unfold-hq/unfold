On your phone, Unfold offers once to tell you when work needs you or is ready for review, and a notice opens that Work Item, not Now.

Notifications are now on for everyone by default, but nothing is sent until you choose a channel: tap **Turn on** on Now, or choose one under **Settings › Notifications**. **Not now** is remembered in that browser. A Web Push notice opens the Work Item or session it is about even when the server has no `baseUrl`, and opens a new window when the open one cannot be moved.
